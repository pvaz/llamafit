# LlamaFit Contributor Licence Agreement

**Version 1.0, 10 September 2026.**

LlamaFit is published under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later. That
covers everybody who receives the software. This page covers something narrower: what happens
to a contribution you send *to* the project.

It exists for one reason. The AGPL does not suit every organisation, and Paulo Vaz, who owns
the copyright in LlamaFit, wants to keep the option of selling a commercial licence to people
who cannot accept it. That is only possible if he holds the necessary rights in **every** line
of the project, including yours. If a single contribution were AGPL-only to him as well, the
whole project would be locked to the AGPL and that option would be gone for good.

So: you keep your copyright, and you give him a broad enough licence that he can go on
licensing LlamaFit as a whole, on the AGPL and on other terms. Nothing here takes your work
away from you, and nothing here removes it from the AGPL release everybody else gets.

Please read it. If a term does not work for you, open an issue and say so before you write the
code; it is much easier to discuss beforehand.

## 1. Who this is between

"You" is the person or organisation submitting the contribution. "The owner" is Paulo Vaz, the
copyright holder of LlamaFit. "Contribution" means any work of authorship you deliberately
submit to the project, in any pull request, patch, issue or message meant to be included:
code, tests, catalog entries, GPU data, translations, documentation, fixtures.

## 2. You keep your copyright

You are not assigning anything. You remain the owner of your contribution and you can use it,
publish it, sell it or relicense it elsewhere, exactly as you could before. This agreement adds
a licence; it takes nothing back.

## 3. The copyright licence you grant

You grant the owner a licence over your contribution that is worldwide, royalty-free,
non-exclusive, perpetual and irrevocable, to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, publicly
perform and display, distribute your contribution and works derived from it, and to
**sublicense these rights, on any terms, including terms that are not the AGPL**, through any
number of tiers.

That last clause is the whole point of this document. It is what lets the owner ship your
contribution both as part of AGPL LlamaFit and as part of a commercially licensed LlamaFit. It does not let anybody take your contribution *out* of the
AGPL release. Whatever the owner sells alongside it, the AGPL version stays published, and your
contribution stays in it.

If you start patent litigation claiming that LlamaFit or a contribution to it infringes a
patent, the patent licences granted to you under this agreement end on the day you file.

By contributing, you say that each of these is true:

1. **It is yours to give.** You wrote the contribution yourself, or you otherwise hold the

2. **Your employer is not in the way.** If you wrote it as part of your job, or using an
   employer's equipment or time, and your employer therefore has rights in it, you have their

3. **You did not import somebody else's work silently.** Where the contribution contains

4. **No numbers from memory.** Every fact in a data file or in documentation carries a primary

If you are contributing on behalf of a company and you have authority to bind it, say so in
the pull request; the agreement is then with the company as well as with you.

## 7. How to agree

Add this line to the description of your first pull request:

```
I have read CLA.md and I agree to it, for this and my future contributions to LlamaFit.
```

That is all. The pull request template carries it as a checklist item. Agreement covers your
later pull requests too, so you write the line once. If your circumstances change (you take a
job with a claim on your work, say), tell the maintainers before your next contribution.

If you would rather sign something explicit, or you are contributing on behalf of a company
whose legal team wants a countersigned document, open an issue and ask.

## 8. If this agreement changes

A new version applies only to contributions made after it is published. Contributions you have
already made stay under the version that was current when you made them, which the git history
of this file records.
